# Outreach Templates

Fill-in-the-blank starting points. Always customize with real research before sending.

## Cold Email

**Subject options:**
- `[Their company] + [your offer area]` — e.g., "Acme Corp + proposal automation"
- Reference their content — e.g., "Your post on client retention"
- Specific problem — e.g., "Cutting onboarding time for agencies"

**Body:**
```
Hi [First name],

[1 sentence that proves you did homework — reference their product, recent post, hire, or win.]

[1 sentence: what you do + why it's relevant to THEM specifically, not generically.]

[1 sentence CTA: low-friction ask — 15-min call, quick question, or yes/no reply.]

[Your name]
[Title, Company]
```

**Example:**
```
Hi Sarah,

Saw you recently launched a self-serve onboarding flow — impressive that you're scaling without
adding support headcount.

We help SaaS teams cut first-week churn by building automated onboarding sequences that adapt
to how each user actually behaves — which might be relevant as you push further into self-serve.

Would a 15-min call next week make sense, or is this not a priority right now?

Alex
Growth Lead, Onboardly
```

## Follow-Up Email (3–5 days later)

```
Hi [First name],

Just bumping this up — wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.

Happy to share a quick example of what we did for [similar company type] if it'd be useful.

Worth a 15 min?

[Your name]
```

## LinkedIn DM (First Touch)

Keep it short. No pitch. End with a question.

```
Hi [Name] — [1 sentence proving you read their content or noticed something specific about them].

[1 genuine question related to that observation — not about your product.]
```

**Example:**
```
Hi Marcus — your recent post on why most agency retainers fail hit close to home for a lot of
people we work with.

Curious: do you find the pricing model is usually the issue, or is it more about scope definition?
```

## LinkedIn DM (Follow-Up / Soft Pitch)

Only send after they engage or reply.

```
Thanks for the reply — [1 sentence acknowledging what they said].

We actually help [their type of company] with exactly this. [1 sentence on what you do.]

Would it be worth a quick call to see if there's a fit?
```

## WhatsApp / SMS (warm leads only)

```
Hey [Name], [Your name] here — [context for why you're messaging].

[1 sentence relevant hook or ask.]

[CTA: simple yes/no or reply to continue]
```

## Channel-Specific Formatting Notes

**Email:**
- Plain text converts better than HTML for cold outreach
- Keep under 100 words for first touch
- One link max (your calendar or website)

**LinkedIn:**
- Character limit: 300 for connection requests, 8000 for DMs
- No attachments on first touch
- Mobile-first: short paragraphs, no walls of text

**WhatsApp:**
- Only for warm leads (they know you or opted in)
- Keep it conversational, not salesy
- Respond within the same session if they reply
