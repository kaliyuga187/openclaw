# Content Format Guide

Detailed specs and examples for each short-form output format.

## Tweets / X Posts (standalone)

- Max 280 characters
- No hashtags unless the creator normally uses them
- Hook in the first 5 words — assume nothing comes before it
- Formats that work: bold claim, counterintuitive take, "X things I learned", short story opening
- Write 5 variants with different angles; let the creator pick

**Example:**
> Most agencies die from too many clients, not too few. The money problem is actually a focus problem.

## Twitter / X Thread

- 6–10 tweets, numbered (1/, 2/, 3/ etc.)
- Tweet 1: the hook — strongest claim or most surprising insight
- Tweets 2–8: one idea per tweet, each self-contained
- Final tweet: summary or CTA (follow, reply, share)
- Each tweet ≤280 characters
- Add a line break between tweets for readability

**Thread structure:**
```
1/ [Hook — big claim or surprising insight]

2/ [Context or first supporting point]

3/ [Second point]

...

N/ [Takeaway + CTA]
```

## LinkedIn Post

- 150–300 words
- First line: hook that works without "see more" expansion
- Format: hook → 2–3 short paragraphs → takeaway → optional CTA
- Line breaks every 1–3 sentences (LinkedIn renders white space well)
- No bullet walls; use them sparingly (3 max)
- End with a question or soft CTA ("What's your take?" / "DM me if this resonates")
- No hashtag spam; 2–3 relevant tags max at the bottom if the creator uses them

**Example structure:**
```
[One-line hook that creates curiosity or makes a bold claim.]

[2–3 sentences expanding on the hook — the "why this matters".]

[The core insight or story beat — the meat of the post.]

[Takeaway: what they should think/do/remember.]

[Optional: question or CTA]
```

## Email Newsletter

- Subject line: write 3 options, let creator pick
  - Option 1: curiosity gap ("The mistake most consultants make with proposals")
  - Option 2: direct/specific ("How I cut proposal time from 4 hours to 20 minutes")
  - Option 3: personal/story ("I almost lost a $15k client because of this")
- Preview text: 1 sentence that complements the subject
- Body: 200–400 words, conversational, first-person
- One main idea only — don't cram in multiple topics
- CTA at the end: one link, one ask

**Structure:**
```
Subject: [Option 1]
Subject: [Option 2]
Subject: [Option 3]
Preview: [One-sentence teaser]

---

[Opening: short, hooks directly into the topic]

[Body: the insight, story, or how-to — 2–3 paragraphs]

[Takeaway: what to remember or do]

[CTA: one specific link or ask]

[Sign-off]
```

## Instagram Caption

- 125 characters for the visible part (before "more")
- Hook must land in those 125 characters
- After the hook: expand with 2–3 short sentences
- Hashtags: 5–10, at the end or in first comment depending on creator preference
- Format: hook → body → hashtags

**Example:**
```
Most people write content. Few people write content that converts.

Here's the difference: [expand in body]

#contentmarketing #copywriting #socialmedia #creatoreconomy
```

## Short-Form Hook Lines

- One sentence, punchy, standalone
- Use as: opening line for any format, caption, or standalone post
- Write 3 with different tones: bold claim / story / question

**Examples:**
- Bold: "The best content calendar is no content calendar."
- Story: "I deleted my editorial calendar and tripled my output."
- Question: "What if the reason your content isn't landing has nothing to do with the content?"
