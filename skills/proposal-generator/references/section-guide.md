# Proposal Section Guide

Detailed guidance for writing each section of a proposal or SOW.

## 1. Cover / Header

Include:
- Your name and company
- Client name and company
- Date
- Project title (specific, not generic — "E-commerce Checkout Redesign" not "Design Project")
- Version number if applicable (v1.0)

## 2. Executive Summary

2–3 sentences. Answer:
- What is the project?
- What problem does it solve or opportunity does it capture?
- What's the outcome the client can expect?

Write this last — it's easier once the full proposal is done.

**Example:**
> This proposal outlines a six-week engagement to redesign Acme Corp's checkout flow, reducing
> cart abandonment and increasing conversion rate. We'll deliver wireframes, three rounds of
> design iteration, and a final Figma handoff package ready for development.

## 3. Scope of Work

Bulleted list of everything included. Be specific and quantified where possible.

- Use active verbs: "Design," "Deliver," "Provide," "Conduct"
- Be specific: "3 rounds of revisions" not "revisions as needed"
- Group by phase or workstream if the project is complex

**Example:**
> - Kickoff workshop (90 min, remote)
> - Current-state audit of existing checkout flow
> - 3 wireframe concepts for mobile and desktop
> - Up to 2 rounds of revisions per wireframe
> - Final high-fidelity Figma file with design system components
> - Handoff documentation for development team

## 4. Out of Scope

Explicitly list what's NOT included. This is as important as scope.

**Common out-of-scope items:**
- Development / implementation
- Copywriting or content creation
- Third-party tool setup or licensing
- Revisions beyond the stated number
- Work outside the defined timeline
- Additional phases or features not in this proposal

## 5. Deliverables

Concrete list of outputs: what the client receives, in what format, and when.

| Deliverable | Format | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff summary | PDF / Google Doc | Day 3 |
| Wireframes (v1) | Figma link | Week 2 |
| Final Figma file | Figma link + export | Week 6 |
| Handoff doc | Notion / Google Doc | Week 6 |

## 6. Timeline

Options depending on project structure:

**Simple (milestone table):**

| Milestone | Week |
|---|---|
| Kickoff + discovery | Week 1 |
| Wireframes delivered | Week 2–3 |
| Revisions + refinement | Week 4–5 |
| Final delivery | Week 6 |

**Complex (phase breakdown):**

Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)
Phase 2: Design (Weeks 2–4)
Phase 3: Refinement (Week 5)
Phase 4: Handoff (Week 6)

Note: Include "Timeline assumes [X]" if it depends on client responsiveness or approvals.

## 7. Investment

Show line items, not just a total. Builds trust and reduces negotiation.

**Fixed price example:**

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Kickoff + discovery | $1,500 |
| Wireframe design (3 concepts) | $3,500 |
| Revisions (2 rounds) | $1,500 |
| Final Figma + handoff | $2,000 |
| **Total** | **$8,500** |

**Payment schedule:**
- 50% due upon signing
- 50% due upon final delivery

**Hourly example:**

| Role | Rate | Est. Hours | Est. Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy | $200/hr | 5 | $1,000 |
| Design | $150/hr | 40 | $6,000 |
| Project management | $100/hr | 10 | $1,000 |
| **Total estimate** | | **55** | **$8,000** |

Note: hourly proposals need a not-to-exceed cap or clients will push back.

## 8. Terms

Include these by default; customize as needed:

- **Revisions**: Number of revision rounds included; additional revisions billed at $X/hr
- **Kill fee**: If client cancels after work begins, X% of remaining balance is due
- **IP / ownership**: Client owns all deliverables upon final payment; work-in-progress remains property of [Your Company] until paid
- **Confidentiality**: Both parties agree to keep project details confidential
- **Timeline dependencies**: Timeline assumes client provides feedback within X business days
- **Governing law**: [State/Country]

## 9. Next Steps

Tell the client exactly what to do. Don't leave it vague.

**Examples:**
> To move forward, simply reply "approved" to this email and we'll send the contract and invoice.
>
> Questions? Schedule a 20-min call here: [calendar link]
>
> This proposal is valid for 14 days.

## Common Pricing Structures

| Structure | Best for |
|---|---|
| Fixed price | Well-defined scope, faster to sell |
| Hourly with cap | Exploratory or research-heavy projects |
| Retainer | Ongoing work, monthly recurring |
| Value-based | Measurable ROI (e.g., conversion rate lift) |
| Phased | Large projects, reduces client risk |
